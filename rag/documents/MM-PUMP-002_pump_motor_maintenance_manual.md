# Pump and Drive Motor Maintenance Manual

## 1. Scope

This manual covers routine and corrective maintenance of centrifugal pumps and their electric drive motors,
including boiler feed pumps, condensate pumps and crude charge pumps.

## 2. Maintenance intervals

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Vibration route measurement | Monthly |
| Lube oil sample and analysis | Quarterly |
| Motor bearing regreasing | Every 4,000 running hours |
| Coupling alignment check | Annually and after any motor or pump removal |
| Bearing replacement (preventive) | 32,000 running hours or on condition |

## 3. Lubrication

Use the lubricant grade on the equipment data plate. Over-greasing a motor bearing raises its temperature
as much as under-greasing. After regreasing, run the motor and confirm bearing temperature settles below
75 degC within two hours.

## 4. Coupling alignment

Misalignment between the motor and the pump is a leading cause of combined motor bearing heating and pump
vibration. Typical signs are:

- the drive motor drive-end bearing temperature rises 5 to 15 minutes before pump vibration alarms;
- high axial vibration and a strong component at twice running speed;
- coupling element wear or rubber dust under the coupling guard.

Alignment tolerance for feed pump couplings is 0.05 mm parallel offset and 0.05 mm per 100 mm angular.
Always re-check alignment after motor replacement, after foundation work, and when the motor bearing and pump
vibration alarms occur together repeatedly.

## 5. Vibration analysis and bearing inspection

1. Isolate the pump and motor according to SAF-005 before any hands-on work.
2. Record overall vibration, spectrum and phase at all bearing positions.
3. Inspect the coupling for wear and check alignment with a laser alignment tool.
4. Check bearing clearances and inspect races for spalling or discoloration.
5. Take a lube oil sample for particle count and water content.
6. Replace bearings that show spalling, discoloration or clearance above the limit in section 6.
7. Record findings in the maintenance system against the asset id.

## 6. Bearing replacement criteria

Replace a bearing when any of the following applies: radial clearance exceeds 0.12 mm, visible spalling,
overheating discoloration, or running hours exceed 32,000 with a rising vibration trend.

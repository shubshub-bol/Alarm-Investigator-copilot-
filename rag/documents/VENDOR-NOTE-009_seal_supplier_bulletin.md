# Seal Supplier Technical Bulletin

## 1. Cartridge seal storage

Store spare cartridge seals flat, in their original packaging, away from sunlight and ozone sources.
Shelf life is five years from the date of manufacture.

## 2. Installation note

Tighten gland nuts evenly in a cross pattern to the torque on the seal drawing. Remove the setting clips
only after the gland is fully tightened.

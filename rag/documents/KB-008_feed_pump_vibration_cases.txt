KNOWLEDGE ARTICLE KB-008: BOILER FEED PUMP VIBRATION - PAST CASES AND RESOLUTIONS

CASE 1 - BFP-102, MARCH 2025 - COUPLING MISALIGNMENT AFTER MOTOR REPLACEMENT
Symptom: repeated high vibration alarms on BFP-102 over three weeks. In most events the drive motor M-102
drive-end bearing temperature alarm came 8 to 14 minutes before the pump vibration alarm.
Root cause: the motor was replaced in February 2025 and the coupling was not laser-aligned afterwards.
Parallel offset was 0.21 mm against a 0.05 mm tolerance.
Resolution: laser realignment and coupling element replacement. No further vibration alarms in 60 days.

CASE 2 - BFP-101, NOVEMBER 2024 - CAVITATION FROM LOW DEAERATOR LEVEL
Symptom: vibration alarms together with low suction pressure alarms, mainly at high load.
Root cause: makeup water valve sticking, causing low deaerator level and cavitation.
Resolution: makeup valve overhaul. Vibration returned to normal once suction pressure was stable.

CASE 3 - BFP-101, JUNE 2023 - BEARING WEAR AT END OF LIFE
Symptom: slowly rising vibration over two months, then frequent alarms. Bearing at 33,500 running hours.
Resolution: bearing replacement during a planned changeover to BFP-102.

LESSONS LEARNED
- Motor bearing heating that leads pump vibration by several minutes points to coupling alignment.
- Vibration together with low suction pressure points to the deaerator, not the pump itself.
- Always change over to the standby pump rather than running a vibrating feed pump for long periods.
